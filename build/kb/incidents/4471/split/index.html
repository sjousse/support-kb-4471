How each report was placed

  • Count each report once. The ticket system exports in pages that overlap, so some reports come twice. uniqueById keeps one per id.
  • Cause from the tenant, not from intake. Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The cause comes from the tenant’s locale and booking form (causeOf in causes.ts, tenants in src/lib/data/tenants).
  • A flipped date on a booking-v3 form is the hint. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads the date day first.
  • A one-day move is not a flip. The desk read some reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json): Tuesday shows as Monday, June bookings are a day off, and one meeting happened on two days in Lisbon and Toronto. No day and month swap there. That points at time zones, not the parser, so these reports get their own ticket instead of a footnote.

Why it was one ticket

Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. All three problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so subject matching put them in one ticket. Engineering was meant to split them later; nobody did.

What to do

  1. Fix the shared parser for day-first locales (ticket 1).
  2. Change the booking-v3 date hint to the tenant’s own order (ticket 2).
  3. Look at time-zone handling for invites and reminders (ticket 3).
  4. Close #4471 only when all three are closed.
